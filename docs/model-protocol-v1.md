# Frozen OJ modeling protocol v1

Prepared 2026-11-18. **No model has been fitted under this protocol. No performance or price-response claim is established.** Machine settings: [`configs/model-protocol-v1.json`](../configs/model-protocol-v1.json). This is a local CPU research implementation, not a public training service. Changes to candidates, features, thresholds, splits, or metrics require a new protocol/lineage and independent review.

The complete source was descriptively analyzed before this freeze, including sales totals/outliers. The existing Phase 1 full-source regression tests were also rerun during preparation and decoded all source labels again for contract/arithmetic assertions, without model scoring or candidate selection. The immutable prepared proposal marks the prospective runner-access freeze after those disclosed audits. Confirmation is therefore prospectively reserved from model selection; it is **not never-read or blind data**. From this freeze, the runner records decoded target accesses and never decodes weeks 153–160 during preparation, smoke or selection. Hashing the complete pinned file and reading target-free keys necessarily touches the file bytes; that is distinguished from decoding sales labels. Source checksum: `3fc53cf6b14df1d69aba1977a27953400e53df5483c23493a38d6031b27b2c54`. No sample filtering or outlier removal is allowed.

## Scientific questions and boundaries

1. Can pooled models improve four-week recorded-sales predictions over fixed recent-mean and seasonal controls? **Primary evaluation is conditional at realized heldout price and feature-advertising values.** Those conditions were not necessarily known at the forecast origin, so this is not evidence of deployable forecast accuracy when future plans are unknown.
2. Which series support bounded price-response scenarios? A predictive improvement does not grant pricing eligibility. A Poisson log-link price coefficient is a conditional association, not an identified causal effect. Sales are unrounded `exp(logmove)` floats; the quasi-likelihood mean model does not assert integer observations or Poisson variance.

A separate reusable scenario interface accepts four explicit future price/feature-ad plans plus origin-only history. It never obtains future sales. Holding last observed price/advertising constant is an explicitly labeled scenario, not a known future fact. Currency, costs, inventory, latent demand, actual calendar/holidays, substitutions and realized profit remain unavailable.

## Frozen time access

All 249 store–brand series are retained when visible at an origin. Selection cutoffs **140, 144, 148** target **141–144, 145–148, 149–152**. Confirmation uses **152 → 153–156 and 156 → 157–160**, with expanding-window refits and history updates at 156 allowed **only for the already frozen candidates**. Confirmation carries the frozen demand winner and the separately selected price Poisson, deduplicated if identical: at most four learned refits total (two per origin), plus both frozen baselines. Both confirmation origins must be authorized together; no candidate/threshold choice follows the first result. Confirmation needs a separate reviewed selection artifact and authorization and is not enabled by a smoke approval. Final production refit after confirmation requires another reviewed proposal.

Training examples use origins 44, 48, …, cutoff−4, each targeting the next four weeks. Origin features are frozen for all four horizons; no realized future sales lag enters a forecast. All source records through cutoff may supply history, but incomplete four-week training blocks after the last declared origin are not added. Training rows require an observed target, an origin-visible series, and at least one observed history week in the preceding eight calendar weeks. Missing observations are unknown, not zero. Every exclusion and prediction fallback is counted.

The prediction roster is every origin-visible series × four weeks. Scores use the same observed-target mask for every candidate and baseline. Missing target rows and target-only series born after origin are reported separately. A row with no recent history falls back to its last known sales with `stale_history`; no history produces `insufficient_history`. Such rows remain in catalog coverage. Explicit zero histories remain zero baselines. All-zero training data use an explicit constant-zero model state, without invoking a failing Poisson optimizer.

## Features and actual fitting

Numeric features: `log1p` of the mean sales in the preceding four/eight **calendar** weeks (observed rows only), observed fractions of those windows, horizon/4, source-week trend `(target_week−40)/52`, and sine/cosine of the source week modulo 52. These are nominal index cycles, not holidays or recovered dates. Target price and feature-ad are observed conditions for conditional evaluation, or caller-supplied plans for scenarios. Unknown ad values use a distinct missing indicator.

Poisson uses fitted one-hot series intercepts, fitted one-hot brand intercepts, one log-price column per brand, numeric origin/history features, and advertising. The brand log-price coefficient is the actual fitted shared response. Series and brand intercepts are penalized learned terms; there is no hand-written count-based sharing weight. Two fixed `PoissonRegressor(alpha=0.1/1.0, solver='lbfgs', max_iter=500, tol=1e-4)` candidates. No numeric scaler is used: the explicit log1p, /4 and /52 transformations above are fixed; brand log-price columns are unscaled and their coefficients need no back-transform. One-hot categories are learned only from the fold training design. Unknown categories never manufacture an independent response.

Tree comparison uses the same encoded features and `HistGradientBoostingRegressor(loss='poisson', max_iter=80, max_leaf_nodes=15, min_samples_leaf=30, learning_rate=0.08, l2_regularization=1, early_stopping=False, random_state=20261118)`. It is a forecast comparator only; this protocol never grants it elasticity or model-pricing eligibility. Fixed iterations avoid a random internal validation split.

Baseline `last4mean` averages observed weeks origin−3…origin; if absent, use observed origin−7…origin, then last observed sales, all explicitly flagged. `seasonal52` uses the exact target week−52 only if observed and known at origin; otherwise it uses `last4mean`. Seasonal support and fallback counts are retained. No Croston tuning, ensemble, bootstrap, additional seeds, demographic features, or causal estimator in v1.

## Metrics, selection and price qualification

Primary: mean absolute error over observed series-week targets. Auxiliary: WAPE = sum absolute error / sum actual sales (null at zero denominator), signed mean error (prediction minus actual), per-series and per-horizon metrics, macro-series MAE and volume groups. Volume groups are thirds by origin training-history mean sales, with deterministic series-ID tie breaks; they never use validation volume. Also report sparse history (<52 observed weeks), new history (<13), and all-zero history coverage. Global MAE pools validation rows; fold results and the worst fold remain visible.

Recent mean is the control. A challenger must improve pooled MAE by ≥2%, beat recent mean in ≥2/3 folds, and have no fold MAE above 1.10× the control. Eligible minimum pooled MAE wins; exact ties prefer recent mean, seasonal52, Poisson alpha 1, Poisson alpha 0.1, tree. Otherwise retain recent mean. Price-response model selection uses the same rule restricted to the two Poisson candidates; no eligible Poisson means no automatic model-price suggestions. Keep both successful and failed trials. An incomplete run has no ranking.

Per-series price eligibility additionally requires ≥52 observations, ≥5 distinct prices, max/min price ≥1.10, a Poisson brand response in [−5, −0.05] at every selection fold, fold coefficient spread ≤1.5, and series pooled conditional MAE no worse than recent mean. The current refitted coefficient must also pass the sign/range screen; failure disables pricing without reselection. The current origin must independently pass data support; proposed prices must lie within that series' observed training min/max. Scenario price/ad pairs additionally require at least eight observed rows and three distinct observed prices in the same known feature-ad stratum and a proposed price inside that stratum's observed min/max; unknown or unsupported combinations cannot be used for model pricing. These are conservative engineering screens, not confidence intervals or identification tests. Unsupported/new/long-tail series retain predictions or rules but cannot claim qualified elasticity. Report association and available support even when eligibility fails.

## Bounded execution and approval stages

No automatic fitting in unit tests, import, prepare, or authorize. Preparation freezes target-free ordered row/group membership, exact trial keys, complete source/config/code/lock/environment hashes (including tracked, staged-worktree and untracked Python source bytes), CPU identity and wrapper command. Independent reviewers author external review JSON; the CLI never generates an approval from a name. Authorization consumes a valid review and **stops**. Execution then requires a new independent `execute-only` review bound to that authorization and exact proposal bytes. Reviews expire, include negative permissions and a one-use nonce. One atomic exclusive directory claim burns each execution lineage; no resume or directory reuse.

Bootstrap smoke is **two fits**, both real backends on the complete origin-140 training design and 141–144 validation path. It has no scientific ranking. Proposed memory accounting: raw/metadata/history ≤100 MiB; dense 24k×~270 float64 design ~50 MiB; backend copies/workspaces ≤350 MiB; models/optimizer ≤100 MiB; predictions/checkpoints ≤150 MiB; interpreter/import/object overhead ≤450 MiB; ≥20% margin, provisional total <1.5 GiB. These are estimates, not measurements. Smoke must measure the outer worker cgroup peak and runtime before formal approval.

Selection is **nine fits** (3 candidates × 3 origins), seed 20261118, one fit at a time, ≤2 native threads, CPU only. Target runtime <300 s; external limit 600 s, stop new work at 480 s. Smoke external limit 180 s, soft drain 120 s. External systemd user service uses `MemoryMax=2147483648`, `MemorySwapMax=0`, `CPUQuota=200%`, `RuntimeMaxSec`, `TimeoutStopSec=15`, `KillMode=control-group`, `OOMPolicy=kill`. Wrapper/sensor availability is a hard gate, not a fallback to unrestricted execution. Supervisor polls complete-worker cgroup RSS/peak and service state every 5 s even during blocking native fits; missing supervisor progress for 30 seconds fails closed; trial JSONL flushes reservation, fit timings, train loss and validation metrics. No per-iteration callback is invented for sklearn L-BFGS. Runs ≥10 minutes are out of scope and require richer governance.

Formal preparation requires authenticated smoke artifacts, successful complete backend coverage, zero OOM/resource events, measured peak ×1.25 <2 GiB, and conservative projection (slowest relevant smoke fit + preprocessing/checkpoint cost, × planned fits ×2 uncertainty) <480 s. Failure requires a new lineage and, for model/features/budget changes, protocol review. Fresh resource checks immediately precede each fit. Nonfinite inputs/targets/model values/predictions, convergence warnings, timeout, unavailable sensors, low disk, missing artifacts, unknown files or incomplete trials fail closed. Partial evidence survives, scientific/publication flags remain false. Model checkpoints are trusted local artifacts with hashes, never accepted from user uploads. Selection and confirmation results still need independent review before publication.
