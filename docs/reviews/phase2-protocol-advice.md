# Phase 2 scientific protocol advice

Reviewed 2026-11-18 by the independent protocol-review agent. Scope: the Phase 1 handoff, weekly data contract and source loader/analysis, Phase 2 plan, `docs/model-protocol-v1.md`, `configs/model-protocol-v1.json`, and limited emerging feature/model code inspection. This is early scientific advice, **not implementation verification, execution authorization, or model-quality approval**. No fitting, execution of the modeling pipeline, raw sales inspection, or performance-based configuration choice was performed by this reviewer.

The proposed conditional recorded-sales task is coherent. Three shared selection origins (140, 144, 148), direct four-week training blocks, fixed origin histories, two regularized pooled Poisson configurations and one bounded tree comparator provide a small, reviewable experiment. Recent mean and exact source-index lag-52 baselines are appropriate controls. The new phase-aware label loader is necessary: the existing `load_oj()` decodes all labels before `as_of()` filters them.

## Requirements before an independent smoke execution review

1. **Keep task claims separate.** Conditional evaluation uses realized heldout prices and feature advertising. A real future four-week plan must receive explicit price/ad scenarios and origin-only history. Neither conditional accuracy nor scenario predictions demonstrate the quality of a forecast with unknown future inputs. Previously completed full-source descriptive EDA must remain disclosed; confirmation is reserved **from** model selection after freeze, not blind or never-read data.
2. **Freeze the complete feature recipe.** Declare scaling/transformation settings, categorical treatment, column order and missing-value rules. Historical training examples must use their own training origin, not a later fold-wide sales summary. Never include `logmove`, which is the transformed label. If log-price columns are standardized, transform fitted coefficients back to log-price units before applying response thresholds. A brand-shared fitted response is not a separately estimated store–brand elasticity.
3. **Prove forecast and access boundaries.** Preparation freezes target-free row membership; the governed loader decodes labels only through the phase's permitted boundary. Distinguish file-byte hashing, key parsing and label decoding accurately. Perturbing later sales or future-born identifiers must not alter earlier features, encoding, grouping, price support, predictions or rosters. Four-week predictions must not consume actual sales from earlier weeks within their horizon. Changing future conditions may affect only explicitly conditional/scenario predictions.
4. **Use common targets and complete coverage.** Every origin-visible series receives a forecast/fallback status for four weeks. All candidates use the same observed-target mask. Report absent targets, after-origin series, recent/seasonal/stale-history fallbacks and failed predictions separately. Missing records never become zero sales. Seasonal lag 52 means an exact known source-index observation, with the declared recent-mean fallback; it does not establish annual or holiday seasonality.
5. **Make evaluation reproducible.** Preserve the predeclared pooled-row MAE promotion rule and deterministic ties. Define signed error explicitly as prediction minus actual (or the reverse, consistently), and report each fold, horizon, series and training-defined volume/history group, including a macro-series MAE view. WAPE with zero actual sum is unavailable. No incomplete trial set produces a ranking. Three selection windows do not establish statistical significance or broad seasonal generalization.
6. **Keep price eligibility conservative and distinct from forecast selection.** The declared counts, variation, coefficient range/stability and series-error checks are engineering screens, not identification tests. Require at least eight observed rows and three distinct prices in the requested known advertising state, and a proposed price inside that state's observed range. These thresholds are not precision guarantees. The current refitted Poisson must also pass coefficient sign/range checks, including at origin 156. Failure disables its price eligibility without triggering reselection. The tree remains a forecast comparator. No unsupported series receives an independent elasticity claim.
7. **Freeze confirmation exactly.** Both origins, 152→153–156 and 156→157–160, are one predeclared confirmation operation. Specify the exact frozen forecast winner and any separate price-response model, baseline roster, deduplication and maximum refit count. The allowed update through week 156 must not change candidates, thresholds or reporting after first-origin results. Current-origin support may be recomputed by the frozen rule. Confirmation and any subsequent full-data refit require their own reviews; smoke cannot authorize them.
8. **Preserve source meaning.** Model unrounded reconstructed positive OJ sales, without invented availability, dates, costs, latent demand or audited revenue. Unknown gaps do not validate an intermittent-demand model. No causal effect, realized profit uplift or uncertainty interval follows from a regularized coefficient or sign screen. The Poisson log link and L2 penalty support the stated mean-model interpretation; its distributional variance is not established by this source. [Official Poisson API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.PoissonRegressor.html)

The bounded tree must retain explicit `early_stopping=False`: the library's automatic setting can enable an internal validation split above 10,000 observations. Freeze the tree's loss, seed and iteration limit as proposed. [Official histogram boosting API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.HistGradientBoostingRegressor.html)

The preparation/authorization/execution separation, authenticated real-backend smoke, resource feasibility gate and preservation of failed runs are appropriate governance requirements. Their enforcement and actual smoke behavior remain for a fresh scope-bound review under `model-training-governance`. This advisory note grants no permission to fit, rank, confirm, publish or deploy.
