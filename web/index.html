What this engine does — in plain language

First: rule out impossible prices

Cost and minimum margin set a lower limit. Change limits and an optional market ceiling set the rest of the range. Conflicting rules produce a review request, never a hidden override.

Then: compare what could happen

For each candidate price, calculate sales and contribution profit under three assumed sensitivities. Select the candidate with the highest worst-case profit across those three scenarios. This is not a statistical confidence interval.

Finally: review and test

A calculated proposal is not proven business value. A real rollout needs verified costs, estimated price response, customer and dealer constraints, and a controlled test. This version uses explicit scenarios and no LLM.
